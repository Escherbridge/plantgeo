# Complete-capture retry controls

The shared writer contract permits four packages to omit a retry series. Watersheds
restarts its bounded national reference walk on the next scheduled turn. Botanical
occurrences replays an already transferred archive whose fetch owner has separate
retries. Land context replays an immutable complete BLM capture when publication is
interrupted, while a failed source capture is retried on its next scheduled turn. Crop
cover resumes verified raster tiles and retries the complete admitted annual edition
through its maintenance owner. The contract test requires all three omitted retry
flags to carry explicit reasons for every exempted package.
