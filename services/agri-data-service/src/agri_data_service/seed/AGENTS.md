# Seed-data contract

Seed files may establish governed lookup identity and source-backed definitions, but they may not smuggle demo scores into operational or training data. A national conservation-practice standard can support a practice name, code, definition, and review limitation; it does not support universal climate thresholds, impact ranks, rates, species choices, or implementation instructions.

All strategy seeds remain `draft` until the exact source version, locator, retrieval metadata, and domain review are recorded. `NULL` means not evidenced. Re-seeding changed content clears prior review state so an old approval cannot authorize a new payload.
