# U.S. Drought Monitor data research

- URL: https://droughtmonitor.unl.edu/Data.aspx
- Retrieved: 2026-07-20
- Intent: validate the initial historical drought source for the PlantGeo four-year backfill.

The U.S. Drought Monitor publishes a weekly assessment of U.S. drought and makes historical GIS vector files, KML, WMS, and statistics available. The official metadata states that vector data are the appropriate source when statistics must match U.S. Drought Monitor values; gridded data are mainly for display and cannot be directly matched to the official statistics. Classifications are D0 through D4 with impact information.

Implementation implications: ingest one immutable vector release for each weekly issue date, record the source release and geometry provenance, preserve the native coverage, and derive analysis-cell assignments only as an explicit reproducible transform.
