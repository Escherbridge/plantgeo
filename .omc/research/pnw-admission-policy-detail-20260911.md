L28: Data Usage Policy
L29: The Consortium of Pacific Northwest Herbaria (CPNWH) provides access to collection data from multiple institutions. These institutions have agreed to make their data available for scientific research and for public information and education. Use of any specimen data accessed through the CPNWH web site requires acceptance to the terms and conditions in the following data usage policy:
L30:   1. The quality and completeness of data cannot be guaranteed. Users employ these data at their own risk. Data users are encouraged to personally verify data before use in critical applications.
L31:   2. Data accuracy and content is determined by the individual providers. Data errors should be reported to the original data providers.
L32:   3. As basic facts, the raw specimen data may be freely used without restriction, including for research purposes. However, users should respect restrictions of access to sensitive data including localities for plants of conservation concern.
L33:   4. Users are strongly encouraged to acknowledge the individual providers of the specimen data they use, as well as the National Science Foundation.
L34:   5. CPNWH does not assert intellectual property rights over the data made available through its network.
L35:   6. CPNWH is not liable or responsible for any loss, damage, claim, cost, or expense however it may arise, from an inability to use the PNW Herbaria Portal.

L28: Data Sharing Policy for Data Providers
L29: By sharing data through the Consortium of Pacific Northwest Herbaria (CPNWH), data providers agree to make their data available for scientific research and for public information and education. Data providers must agree to the following terms and conditions:
L30:   1. Specimen data accessible through the CPNWH web site are openly and universally available to all users within the framework of the Data Usage Policy.
L31:   2. CPNWH does not assert intellectual property rights over the data made available through its network. Data providers retain rights to the data they share.
L32:   3. The data provider warrants that they have made the necessary agreements with the original owners of the data (if different) and have obtained rights to make the data available through the CPNWH web site.
L33:   4. The data provider makes reasonable efforts to ensure that the data they serve are accurate.
L34:   5. CPNWH restricts access to sensitive data through its web site; however, the provider of the data may also wish to block such sensitive data within the dataset they provide to CPNWH.
L35:   6. The data provider should provide a stable and unique identifier for each record in their data so that the owner of the data can be known.
L36:   7. CPNWH caches, on its web server, a copy of the data made available by each provider, to be used in accordance with the terms and conditions set forth by the data provider.
L37:   8. CPNWH is not liable or responsible for the data contents, or for any loss, damage, claim, cost or expense however it may arise, from an inability to use the PNW Herbaria Portal.
