lane registry and acquire separate provisioning coverage checks. Watersheds is
still registered as `static_lookup` with a source-direct watermark and refusal
adapter. Successful direct writing does not, by itself, supersede that literal
architecture obligation.

generic registry/provisioning obligation with retirement's product/source proof
and reader's serving acceptance, or identify the authority that supersedes it.
Until that acknowledgement is recorded, item G remains in this umbrella. Planned
successors above identify scope ownership; they do not establish delivered products.

production-acceptance handoffs. Botanical source admission and any future
