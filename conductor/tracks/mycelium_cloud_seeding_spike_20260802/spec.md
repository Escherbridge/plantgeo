# Mycelium cloud-seeding product spike (natural-transport bioprecipitation)

## Goal

Test the feasibility of commercial products — soil amendments, growth media,
and animal-feed inoculants — that embed ice-nucleation-active (INA) fungal
spores and rely on *natural transport* (wind erosion, rain splash, compost
convection, gut-passage/dung cycling) to deliver those spores to clouds, per
the bioprecipitation feedback hypothesis. The deliverable of this track is a
validated-or-killed verdict per spike question, not a product, field trial, or
weather-modification activity.

## Evidence boundary

- All inputs are published literature (see References) or clearly labeled
  estimates. No measurements exist yet; none may be fabricated.
- This track authorizes no acquisition, culture, or release of any organism.
- No precipitation-efficacy, weather-modification, or agronomic-performance
  claim may be published from spike outputs. Marketing language derived from
  this work must remain agronomic (see spike 005).
- Dose and attribution math are decision inputs with stated assumptions, not
  measurements.
- No Railway mutation, production data change, or shared-service modification.

## Established findings (research session 2026-08-02)

- Fungal spores are the dominant warm-temperature ice nucleating particles in
  a year-long sub-Arctic atmospheric study, and are locally emitted
  (ACP 25:12007, 2025).
- *Mortierella alpina*, a ubiquitous soil saprobe already mass-cultured
  industrially: all INA isolates initiated freezing at -5 to -6 C; nuclei are
  proteinaceous; spores present in air and rain (BG 12:1057, 2015).
- Species selection is decisive: *Cladosporium* (the most abundant atmospheric
  spore) is a poor IN due to hydrophobin surface coating (ACP 11:1191, 2011);
  mushroom/smut/mold spores show low active fractions at cold temperatures
  (ACP 14:8611, 2014).
- Agricultural soil dusts may be 20-25% of the global atmospheric dust load;
  organic-rich particles dominate their ice nucleation (ACP 14:8521, 2014).
  Farming already injects fungal/organic INP at continental scale; an
  amendment product upgrades the IN quality of an existing pathway.
- Soils rich in biological INP abound in ice-nucleating macromolecules likely
  produced by fungi (BG 15:4381, 2018).
- Bioprecipitation feedback framework: landscapes influence precipitation via
  hosted microbes; land management is an inadvertent lever (GCB 20:341, 2014).
- Fungal ice-nucleating proteins identified; explicit "engineered weather"
  framing (Virginia Tech / Wang & Vinatzer, 2026-03).
- Commercial precedent for deployed biological IN: Snomax (P. syringae
  protein) in snowmaking.
- Coprophilous fungi prove the feed-through dispersal engine: spores survive
  gut passage, fruit on dung, and re-disperse ballistically.

## Product concept under test

- **P1 Soil amendment (flagship):** INA consortium on biochar/compost granule;
  sold on soil-health value; hydroseeding/reclamation channels for scale.
- **P2 Growth media:** potting mixes, green-roof substrate, reforestation
  inoculant packs (emission decks on windward slopes and urban rooftops).
- **P3 Animal feed:** gut-surviving INA spores; dung pats become distributed
  rangeland sporulation nodes. Highest R&D burden (survival assays).
- **P4 Municipal compost channel:** compost facilities as semi-controlled,
  thermally self-lofting emission nodes.

Revenue derives from agronomic value; the atmospheric effect is an externality.
Later phases could add watershed adoption contracts (PES-style) and
attribution data services, which is where PlantGeo fits as operating map
(adoption ledger, emission inventory, corridor siting, trial registry,
monitoring dashboard).

## Acceptance criteria

- Each executed spike closes with a README verdict: VALIDATED, PARTIAL, or
  INVALIDATED, with evidence. An INVALIDATED 001 is a successful spike and
  likely closes the track.
- 001/002 computations state every assumption and cite published ranges for
  background INP, sporulation rates, and lofting fractions.
- 004 excludes plant pathogens and known allergens by rule, not by judgment.
- 005 names statutes and produces a written permitted/forbidden claims list.
- No spike output authorizes organism work, field activity, or product claims.
- Spike artifacts live under this track's directory, are dated, and are not
  edited into new conclusions after the fact.
