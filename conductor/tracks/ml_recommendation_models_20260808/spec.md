# ML recommendation models: species fit and strategy selection

## Goal

Build the dedicated ML lane of the agri-data-service: a governed, citation-bound
**expert recommendation label plane** harvested from trusted published sources,
a **k-NN analog (AnEn) forecast method** joining the existing MC and ridge
forecasters, **conformal self-correction** of forecast bands against recorded
actuals, and two supervised models trained on the expert labels:

- **Model A — species fit.** Given a planting region's governed feature vector
  (signal streams, MC forecast statistics, ML forecast statistics, k-NN analog
  statistics, seasonality), return a ranked list of best-fit plant species for
  the region's problems and advantages, with wildfire prevention and water
  conservation as first-class scoring objectives, not post-hoc filters.
- **Model B — strategy selection.** Over the same feature space, select among
  agricultural deployment methods — reforestation, agroforestry, silvopasture,
  alley cropping, windbreaks/shelterbelts, riparian buffers, managed grazing,
  cover cropping, and peers — treating agriculture as a land, weather, moisture,
  and wildfire management tool.

All code lives in `services/agri-data-service` and follows
`conductor/code_styleguides/sql.md` and `python.md`.

## The claim boundary (load-bearing)

Literature-derived labels are **expert recommendation labels** — "what a
trusted source recommends under these conditions" — not intervention outcome
labels. They therefore:

- get their **own additive label plane**; they never enter the `0013`
  strategy-selection causal plane, whose custody preflight
  (`strategy_label_mapping.py`) rightly demands treatment/control intervention
  evidence that these labels are not. That plane stays blocked on its own terms.
- produce models whose outputs are claim-tiered as **literature-grounded
  recommendations with citations**, never causal effect claims. Nothing in this
  track finalizes an `effect_candidate` or touches the retained abstention
  gates.
- carry full provenance per label row: source work, edition/version,
  section/page locator, source URL where one exists, jurisdiction and climate
  context of the recommendation, licence posture, and reviewer sign-off.
  Structured facts and recommendations are encoded; source prose is not
  reproduced beyond short attributed locators.

## Label programme

Labels are generated by an orchestrated multi-agent workflow (explicitly
authorized) that extracts (site-condition envelope → recommended
species/practices) tuples from trusted sources, at minimum:

1. Mark Shepard, *Restoration Agriculture* (New Forest Farm practice as the
   worked example of broadacre permaculture/keyline in a continental climate).
2. USDA NRCS Conservation Practice Standards (agroforestry, silvopasture,
   windbreak, riparian, prescribed grazing CPS codes).
3. SARE research bulletins and university extension publications.
4. Savanna Institute and comparable agroforestry research/demonstration sites
   ("similar research locations") whose conditions can be mapped into our
   feature space.

Each extracted label binds a condition envelope (climate normals, moisture
regime, soil texture/drainage where stated, fire regime, slope/terrain where
stated) that is then **evaluated against our governed streams** to produce
feature-space training rows. Labels enter as `draft` and become trainable only
after review approval, mirroring the existing strategy-record review contract.

## Method and framework decisions

- **Framework: scikit-learn + numpy** (new pinned dependency `scikit-learn`).
  Model classes are restricted to those whose fitted state is fully
  JSON-extractable, because the repo's artifact rule is canonical JSON, never
  pickle: k-NN (hyperparameters + governed-data reference, index rebuilt
  deterministically), linear/logistic (coefficient vectors), and tree ensembles
  only via explicit structure export if a phase proves they earn their keep.
- **k-NN forecasting is the Analog Ensemble method** (Delle Monache et al.
  2013; feature weighting per Junk et al. 2015): neighbors searched over the
  pinned covariate vector with a **temporal exclusion window** around the query
  origin, empirical quantiles from the analogs' successors, scored against
  `daily_increment_bootstrap_v1` at identical rolling origins on the
  wind-lane's receipt chain (`forecast_training_run`/`forecast_backtest_metric`,
  method `ml`).
- **Self-correction is split-conformal calibration** over the already-recorded
  `forecast_iteration_actual` residuals, availability-gated so no residual is
  used before its database-recorded arrival; analog-space bias correction
  (mean residual of the k nearest analogs) rides the same plumbing.
- **Feature growth is a schema version.** MC-forecast statistics, ML-forecast
  statistics, k-NN analog statistics, and seasonality terms enter training only
  through `agri_covariates_v2`, authored as one reviewed change that also moves
  as-of gating from the global cutoff to per-issue-date, closing the documented
  revision-leakage gap.
- **Serving-side neighbor search may use pgvector** (already a dependency);
  training-side search stays in numpy/scikit-learn for determinism.

## Invariants

- SQL follows the standard: every DDL object once in `db/agri/**` via
  forward-only Alembic + `regenerate.py` with the parity test green; every
  non-trivial runtime query in `src/agri_data_service/sql/<package>/<name>.sql`
  with a purpose/loader/params header, loaded through `load_query_sql`, bound
  parameters only.
- Evaluation evidence stays out of serving surfaces exactly as today: staged
  runs, absent receipts, and the publication gates are the guarantee.
- Backtests carry honest accounting: rolling origins with non-overlapping
  target spans, effective-sample-size caveats in `validation_metrics`, and
  feature-coverage shrinkage reporting, matching the wind-lane precedent.
- Model artifacts are canonical JSON with recomputable digests; a training run
  binds an existing validated release set and quality policy, minting neither.
- Wildfire-prevention and water-conservation objectives are encoded in the
  label schema and loss/scoring weights, and are visible in every model
  receipt.
- The existing NDVI MC, ridge wind lane, iteration planes, and Conductor
  history remain unchanged and queryable.
- `conductor/` remains an OKF bundle; every new Markdown artifact carries
  `type` frontmatter.
- One sweep: all edits land first, then tests/lint/typecheck/build run once at
  the end of each phase, followed by an independent review pass.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Conformal calibration measurably improves NDVI iteration interval coverage
   toward nominal on held-out origins, with before/after coverage recorded in a
   decision record.
2. The AnEn lane forecasts at least one Boise metric end to end — governed
   reads, temporal-exclusion neighbor search, quantile bands, receipts — and its
   backtest is compared like-for-like against the SQL bootstrap baseline.
3. The expert label plane holds reviewed, citation-complete labels from at
   least the four named source families, queryable with full lineage, with
   zero rows in the `0013` causal plane touched.
4. `agri_covariates_v2` exists as one reviewed schema version adding
   forecast-derived and seasonality features and per-issue-date as-of gating.
5. Model A returns a ranked species list for a Boise-area region with per-item
   citations and wildfire/water objective scores; Model B returns a ranked
   strategy selection over the named methods with the same transparency. Both
   persist canonical-JSON artifacts and training receipts.
6. A serving route exposes both models' outputs labeled as literature-grounded
   recommendations with citations; no output is phrased or typed as a causal
   effect.
7. The integrated sweep (pytest, ruff, mypy, migration parity) passes once at
   the end, followed by an independent quality review.

## Out of scope

- Causal effect estimation, `effect_candidate` finalization, or any write to
  the `0013` strategy-selection label plane.
- LLM narrative synthesis and the RAG retrieval endpoint (remain with
  `rag_recommendation_engine_20260324`; this track supersedes only its ML
  scoring/recommendation internals).
- Economic analysis, parcel-level legal determinations, and life-safety
  wildfire predictions.
- Deep-learning frameworks; anything requiring pickle artifacts.
