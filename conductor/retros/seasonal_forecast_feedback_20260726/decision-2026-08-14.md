# Decision record, 2026-08-14: seasonal candidate ladder and the derived-signal lineage plane

Binds to export `seasonal-boise-2026-08-14`, manifest SHA-256
`e0c99f312c898495787fc1e32d93911a2e06cc849016d4cf9f92b706d5554df0`, scored under

## 1. What was measured, and what the measurement is worth

27 rolling origins per series (16 development, 11 final holdout), 8 target series, 30-day horizon,
non-overlapping target windows, 2,640 scored final-holdout days per candidate. No abstention
threshold fired: target-day coverage was 1.0000 and both fold counts cleared their pre-registered
minimums. 54,216 history slices passed the harness leakage guard, which raises rather than warns.

**These metrics prove the framework runs end to end. They are not an operational forecast and not a
life-safety-valid one.** Two limits are structural, measured, and stated before the numbers:

1. **Not revision honest.** Every Boise-area observation's `data_available_at` is 2026-08-05 or
   later, while the observations run back to 2022-04-30. A 2024 origin therefore reads the 2026
   revision of a 2024 observation. The evaluation is observation-time honest (no value dated on or
   after an origin enters that origin's fit, calibration, scaling or hyperparameter choice) and is
   **not** point-in-time honest. This is the same `as_of_mode = global` limitation the covariate wind
   lane records.
2. **The target is reanalysis, not ground truth.** NASA POWER WS2M and T2M are MERRA-2-derived. A
   good score means "this reproduces MERRA-2's field from its own recent history", not "this
   forecasts weather". No station series exists in this warehouse to validate against.

11 origin blocks the bootstrap resamples, not 88 and certainly not 2,640. MAPE is `null` for every

The pooled MAE mixes m/s with C and is dominated by the temperature series. That is a **defect of the
pre-registration**, not a finding. It was left in force rather than rewritten after the numbers were
seen; `results-2026-08-14.md` carries a per-signal diagnostic table that is explicitly not
decision-bearing, and it shows the same ordering within each unit (ridge best on both; climatology
close behind; `sql_linear` competitive on wind at 0.4484 skill but catastrophic on temperature at
-0.4228, which is what the pooled number and the JJA seasonal gate both catch). A future
pre-registration must group the decision by unit.

## 4. Failed gates and corrections found while executing

- **Two pre-registered reporting slices were not delivered** *(added 2026-08-14
  after independent review)*: the pre-registration (§ metrics) promised metrics
  sliced by meteorological season AND by horizon step; only the season slice
  was implemented (`slice_by_season`) — no per-horizon-step block exists in
  `results.json`. The per-candidate pass fraction is computed (48 entries in
  `results.json`) but was omitted from `results-2026-08-14.md`. Neither
  omission moves any gate decision; both are recorded here as deviations
  rather than silently dropped.
- **Pre-registration arithmetic correction.** Section 3 of the pre-registration named 2026-07-07 as
  the last origin. That is the last calendar date whose 30-day target window still closes inside the
  corpus; the last origin **on the 30-day stride** is 2026-06-18. The pre-registered origin counts
  (27 / 16 / 11) are exactly what the code produced, so nothing downstream moves. Recorded rather
  than silently fixed.
- **Bootstrap defect, found and fixed before any number was quoted.** The first implementation keyed
  the persistence baseline on the origin date alone, which collapsed all eight series and produced a
  non-zero skill interval for `persistence_v1` compared with itself. The sanity check (baseline
  versus itself must be exactly [0, 0]) is what caught it; it is now a regression test
  (`tests/test_seasonal_evaluation.py::test_the_bootstrap_of_a_baseline_against_itself_is_exactly_zero`).
- **Manifest-digest defect, found after it reached persisted receipts.** `read_manifest` recomputed
  the digest from a lossy reconstruction, so the benchmark and the first persistence pass recorded
  `cf387c9e...` - a well-formed digest matching no export. `read_manifest` now carries the digest
  read off disk; the benchmark and the lineage plane were re-run, and the receipts now carry
  `e0c99f31...`, which equals `MANIFEST.sha256` and the recomputed manifest text digest.
- **A constraint that looked redundant is, in fact, implied — kept for a
  clearer refusal.** *(Corrected 2026-08-14 after independent review:)*
  `ck_forecast_lineage_edge_child_not_available_before_parent` IS implied by its
  siblings — the composite FK pins the edge's copies to the real rows,
  `ck_forecast_derived_signal_value_available_after_origin` gives
  `child_avail ≥ child_origin`, and `ck_..._parent_available_at_child_origin`
  gives `parent_avail ≤ child_origin`, hence `child_avail ≥ parent_avail`
  always. The constraint is retained because it produces a distinct, earlier
  refusal; a fixture violating multiple constraints reports whichever
  PostgreSQL evaluates first, so tests should not depend on which name appears.

## 5. What the accepted candidates may be used for

Evaluation and ML-feature evidence only. Concretely, the accepted families may:

- be re-scored on a new frozen export under a new pre-registration;
- have their point forecasts and residual-feedback signals persisted in the `20260814_0021` plane,
  which is `evaluation_only` CHECKed true, `publication_authorized` CHECKed false, has no foreign key
  to any publication, receipt, value or run relation, and is reachable by no view or matview;
- expose those derived signals to an ML feature snapshot **only** through
  `agri.forecast_derived_signal_snapshot_eligible`, which admits a value only when the snapshot is
  `validated` and the value's whole transitive ancestry is available at or before the snapshot's
  training-window end.

They may not become a forecast publication, a serving row, a receipt, a recommendation, a strategy
label, or a scheduled job.
